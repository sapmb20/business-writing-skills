# Finance résumé and cover-letter templates

Part of `career-writing`. A one-page résumé and a cover letter for finance and banking
roles. If the user has a template from a school, club, or firm, use that instead and apply
this skill's bullet rules to it.

> **Source and license.** Written for this repo (CC BY 4.0, see LICENSE-CONTENT). The layout
> follows conventions common to US finance recruiting (one page, reverse chronological,
> education first for students, dates right-aligned, no photo or objective). No
> openly licensed finance résumé template that met this repo's sourcing rule was found, so
> per that rule this one is original. Bullet guidance comes from `career-writing` ("Replace
> skill labels with evidence", STAR).

---

## 1. One-page résumé

Letter size, margins 0.5-1 inch, one serif or sans-serif face, name 16-20 pt, body 10-11 pt.
Put dates and locations on a right-aligned tab stop, not spaces. Single column, standard
headings, no tables or text boxes, so applicant tracking systems can parse it.

```
                              FIRST LAST
          City, ST · (555) 555-0100 · first.last@example.com · linkedin.com/in/firstlast

EDUCATION
University Name                                                            City, ST
B.S. in [Major], Concentration in [Finance]                           Expected May 20XX
• GPA: X.XX/4.00 [include when it helps]; [test scores, if strong and expected]
• Honors: [Dean's List (semesters), scholarships]
• Relevant coursework: [Corporate Finance, Financial Accounting, Investments, ...]

EXPERIENCE
Firm Name                                                                  City, ST
Title, Group                                                         Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY
• [Verb] [what you did] [how / with what], [result with a number], no closing period
• [Built a three-statement model for a $40M add-on acquisition, used in the final IC memo]
• [Two lines at most per bullet; three to five bullets for the most relevant role]

Firm Name                                                                  City, ST
Incoming [Title]                                                          Summer 20XX
                                                         (a role not yet started: no bullets)

LEADERSHIP
Organization                                                               City, ST
Role                                                                 Mon YYYY – Present
• [What you led or changed, and what came of it: members, money raised, events, outcomes]

SKILLS & INTERESTS
Technical: [Excel (financial modeling), Python, Bloomberg, Capital IQ, ...]
Certifications: [e.g. Bloomberg Market Concepts, FMVA]
Languages: [Fluent in X; conversational in Y]
Interests: [Two or three specific ones a reader could ask about]
```

Rules that matter most for finance readers:

- **Evidence, not labels:** every bullet shows a skill through a specific act and result.
- **Numbers:** deal size, dollars, percentages, counts, time saved, wherever true.
- **Reverse chronological** within each section; the most relevant role gets the most room.
- **Mechanics:** consistent date format (Jan 2025 – Sep 2025, spaced en dash); past tense
  for past roles, present for current; no personal pronouns; no closing periods on bullets
  (or periods on all, consistently).
- **Low GPA:** don't drop the line silently if the employer expects one; flag it to the user.

## 2. Cover letter

Under one page; three or four short paragraphs.

```
[Your name]
[City, ST · phone · email]

[Month D, YYYY]

[Recipient's name, title]
[Firm]
[City, ST]

Dear [Mr./Ms. Last name]:

[Why you're writing and why this firm, specifically: the role, how you heard of it (a
named contact, if they agreed), and one concrete reason this firm and group, such as a deal,
a strategy, or a conversation.]

[Proof, part one: your strongest experience as a short STAR story that matches the
posting's top requirement, with a number.]

[Proof, part two: a second skill the posting asks for (technical, leadership, or teamwork),
with evidence, and what you'd bring to the team.]

[The ask: "I would welcome the chance to discuss how I could contribute to [group]. I'm
available [days/times] and can be reached at [phone]." Thanks.]

Sincerely,
[Name]
```

Before sending: the firm's name is right everywhere (a wrong name is the most common fatal
error); every claim matches the résumé; under 400 words.
