# Email and letter templates

Part of `email-writing`. Three fill-in templates: a request email, a follow-up, and a
full-block business letter.

> **Source and license.** Written for this repo (CC BY 4.0, see LICENSE-CONTENT), following
> the conventions in `email-writing`. The full-block layout is the standard US business
> letter format, consistent with the correspondence chapters of open business-writing texts
> such as *Technical Writing Essentials, 2nd Edition* (Last, BCcampus).

---

## 1. Request email

```
Subject: [Action + topic + date], e.g. "Approve Q3 travel budget by Fri, Oct 16"

Hi [First name],                       (or "Dear Ms. [Last name]," for a first contact)

[Main point in one or two sentences: what you need and by when.]

[Context in two or three sentences: only what the reader needs to say yes. Link or attach
the detail rather than pasting it.]

Could you [specific action] by [weekday, date]? [If it helps: "A yes by reply is enough."]

[One line of thanks or goodwill.]

[Sign-off],
[Full name]
[Title, organization]
[Phone]
```

Before sending: one purpose per email; the ask has a verb, an owner (the reader), and a
date; the subject line would make sense in the reader's inbox a week from now.

---

## 2. Follow-up (no reply yet)

Send once, in the same thread, about a week after the first message.

```
Subject: (keep the original subject, so it stays in the thread)

Hi [First name],

Following up on my note below about [topic]. [Restate the ask in one line, with the date.]

[Optional: one new fact that makes it easier to answer, or a smaller ask: "Even a quick
'not now' would help me plan."]

Thanks,
[First name]
```

Don't send a third message in the same week; if it matters, call or ask someone else.

---

## 3. Full-block business letter

Every line starts at the left margin; no indents; single-spaced, with a blank line between
parts.

```
[Your organization's letterhead, or your street address
 City, State ZIP]                                   (omit the name; it goes in the signature)

[Month D, YYYY]

[Recipient's full name, title]
[Organization]
[Street address]
[City, State ZIP]

Dear [Mr./Ms./Dr. Last name]:          (colon in formal US letters; "Dear [Full name]:"
                                        when you don't know how the person is addressed)

[Opening paragraph: the purpose, in the first sentence or two.]

[Body: the details, one topic per paragraph.]

[Closing paragraph: the action you want or will take, with a date; a line of goodwill.]

Sincerely,

[Handwritten signature]

[Full name]
[Title]

Enclosure: [Document name]             (or "Enclosures (2)")
cc: [Name]
```

Use a letter for formal, external, or legal matters; for bad news, structure the body with
`delivering-bad-news` and its templates.
