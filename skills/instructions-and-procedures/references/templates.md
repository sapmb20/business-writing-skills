# Procedure templates

Part of `instructions-and-procedures`. Two fill-in templates: a standard operating procedure
(SOP) and a job description.

---

## 1. Standard operating procedure (administrative)

> **Source and license.** Adapted from U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, *Guidance for
> Preparing Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)*, EPA QA/G-6, EPA/600/B-07/001 (April
> 2007), sections 2.5, 3, and 4.2,
> https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-06/documents/g6-final.pdf. A work of the US
> federal government, in the public domain in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105). The EPA
> does not endorse this adaptation. **Changes:** the EPA's administrative-SOP elements are
> kept in order; the fill-in prompts, the actor/action table, and the finance examples are
> added for this repo.

**Title page**

```
[Organization] — [Division or team]
Standard Operating Procedure

Title:        [Names the activity, e.g. "Vendor payment approval"]
SOP ID:       [e.g. FIN-AP-004]        Revision: [n]        Effective date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Prepared by:  [Name, title, signature, date]
Approved by:  [Name, title, signature, date]
```

**Control line on every later page** (top right): `[SOP ID] | Rev. [n] | [date] | Page [x] of [y]`

**Table of contents** (if the SOP is long, or to show which sections a revision changed)

**Procedures**

```
1. Purpose
   [What the procedure is for, in one or two sentences.]

2. Scope and applicability
   [When it applies, to whom, and what it doesn't cover.]

3. Summary of procedure
   [Three to five sentences, so a reader knows the shape before the steps.]

4. Definitions
   [Terms, acronyms, and system names with a special meaning here.]

5. Responsibilities and qualifications
   [Each role and what it does; required training or authority, e.g. "Approver: a manager
   with signing authority above $5,000".]

6. Procedure
   | Step | Actor | Action | Record left behind |
   | 1 | [Requester] | [Submits ... in ... with ...] | [Ticket number] |
   | 2 | [Approver] | [Reviews against ...; approves or returns within ... days] | [Approval in system] |
   | 3 | [...] | [...] | [...] |
   [Imperative or indicative, one action per step; warnings before the step they apply to.]

7. Criteria and checklists
   [Standards applied during the procedure: limits, required documents, the checklist.]

8. Records management
   [Forms used, where records are filed, how long they are kept.]
```

**Quality control:** [The checks or reviews before the output is accepted, e.g. a second
person reconciles the payment run to approvals; who reviews exceptions.]

**References:** [Related SOPs, policies, and system guides, with revision numbers.]

Keep a master list of all SOPs (ID, revision, date, owner, status) and archive old
revisions so that no one works from an outdated copy.

## 2. Job description

> **Source and license.** Written for this repo (CC BY 4.0, see LICENSE-CONTENT), following
> the four-section structure in `instructions-and-procedures` section 5.

```
Job title:        [Title]
Department:       [Department]
Reports to:       [Title of the manager, not a name]
Status:           [Full-time / part-time; exempt / non-exempt]
Location:         [On-site / hybrid / remote; city]

Scope
[Two or three sentences: what the role is mainly for, its secondary functions, and who
reports to it.]

Duties (most important first; each begins with a verb)
- [Prepare the monthly variance analysis for the operating budget]
- [Reconcile the operating bank accounts weekly]
- [...]
- [Perform other related duties as assigned]   (optional, last)

Requirements
- Education: [Required]; [preferred]
- Experience: [Years and kind]
- Licenses or certifications: [e.g. CPA preferred]
- Skills: [Specific tools and abilities]

[Optional: physical requirements, travel, and the equal-opportunity statement your
organization uses.]
```

Describe the job, not the current holder; list every regular task before ranking them; keep
duties specific enough that a qualified outsider could tell what the job involves.
