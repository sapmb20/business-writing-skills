# Memo template

Part of `memo-writing`. Fill in the brackets; delete any section the memo doesn't need.

> **Source and license.** The heading block and the headed-section body are adapted from
> "7.2 Memos" in *Technical Writing Essentials, 2nd Edition* by Suzan Last (BCcampus),
> https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/technicalwriting2ed/chapter/memos/, licensed
> [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). **Changes:** the original
> sample is a course-assignment memo; this version generalizes the section headings, adds
> the ask-early order and the report-memo variant from `memo-writing` section 3, and adds
> the fill-in guidance.

## The general memo (ask early)

```
MEMO                                                           [Logo]

To:       [Full name, title; or a group, e.g. "All accounts payable staff"]
From:     [Your full name, title]
Date:     [Month D, YYYY]
Subject:  [Specific topic and focus, e.g. "New $5,000 approval limit for vendor payments, effective May 1"]
cc:       [Only people who need it]

[Opening, 1-3 sentences: the purpose and the one thing readers must know.]

[Heading that names the background, e.g. "Why the limit is changing"]
[Context, 3-10 sentences: what happened, why readers are getting this, key facts and dates.]

[Heading that names the action, e.g. "What you need to do"]
Please [task] by [date].
- [Action 1, owner, date]
- [Action 2, owner, date]

[Heading for the details, e.g. "How the new approval route works"]
[Discussion: the details that support the action, most important first.]

[Closing: repeat the action and the date; say who to contact, by name.]

Attached: [Document name], [date]
```

## The report-memo variant (findings first)

Use this order when readers may resist the recommendation and need the evidence first
(`memo-writing` section 4). The heading block is the same.

```
[Opening: the question you were asked, and your bottom line in one sentence.]

Background
[The problem, how it surfaced, and the scope and method: sources, who you spoke to, limits.]

Findings
[Headed subsections, one per finding, strongest first.]

Conclusions
[What the findings mean, not a repeat of them.]

Recommendations
1. [Verb-first action, owner, date]
2. [...]

[Closing: offer to discuss; name the decision you need and by when.]
```

## Adapting it to a report type

| Report type | Change to the template |
| --- | --- |
| Investigative | Findings first; the subject line names the question ("Why Q3 vendor payments ran late") |
| Feasibility | Add a "Criteria" section before Findings, and judge each option against the same criteria |
| Progress | Use `reports-and-proposals/references/templates.md` (progress report) |
| Incident | Use `reports-and-proposals/references/templates.md` (incident report) |
| Activity or status | Replace the body with four short headed sections: current work, problems, next period's plans, staffing |
