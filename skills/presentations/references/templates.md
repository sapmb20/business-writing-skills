# Meeting templates: agenda and minutes

Part of `presentations` (section 9).

> **Source and license.** The default order of business and the essentials that open the
> minutes are adapted from Henry M. Robert, *Robert's Rules of Order for Deliberative
> Assemblies* (S. C. Griggs, 1876): § 44, "Order of Business", and the article "The
> Officers and the Minutes"; Project Gutenberg eBook #9097, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9097. Public domain in the
> United States. **Changes:** modernized wording; added the purpose line, timing, owners, and
> the action-item table for business meetings. For a formal body with bylaws, follow its
> adopted parliamentary authority instead.

---

## 1. Agenda

Send it at least a day ahead, with anything people must read first.

```
[Meeting name]
[Weekday, Month D, YYYY] · [start–end time, time zone] · [room or video link]
Purpose: [The decision or outcome this meeting exists for, in one sentence.]
Attendees: [Names]   Chair: [Name]   Minutes: [Name, not the chair]
Pre-read: [Document, link]

1. Approval of the last meeting's minutes                      [2 min]
2. Reports                                                     [10 min]
   a. [Standing report, presenter]
   b. [Committee or project report, presenter]
3. Unfinished business                                         [15 min]
   a. [Item carried over: decision needed]
4. New business                                                [20 min]
   a. [Item: discussion or decision? presenter]
5. Action items review and next meeting date                   [5 min]
```

Put decisions early enough that time can't run out on them, and mark each item as
information, discussion, or decision.

---

## 2. Minutes

Record what was **done**, not what was said: decisions, motions and their results, and
assignments.

```
[Organization or team] — Minutes of the [regular / special] meeting
[Date], [place or "by video"], [start time]

Present: [Names]   Absent: [Names]   Chair: [Name]   Recorder: [Name]

1. Minutes. The minutes of [date] were [approved as circulated / approved as corrected:
   correction].
2. Reports. [Report title, presenter]: [one-line summary]. [Any action taken on it.]
3. Unfinished business. [Item]: [decision, or "deferred to [date]"].
4. New business. [Item]: [Motion by [name], seconded by [name], to [text of motion].
   Carried [votes for–against] / Failed.]

Action items
| Action | Owner | Due |
| [Verb-first action] | [One named person] | [Date] |

Next meeting: [date, time, place]. Adjourned at [time].

[Recorder's name], Recorder          [Chair's name], Chair (if required)
```

Circulate the minutes within a day or two, while memories are fresh, and carry open action
items onto the next agenda.
