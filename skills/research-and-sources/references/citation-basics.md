Part of `research-and-sources` (step 7). APA 7 reference models for common source types,
models for finance sources, and one source in three styles.

## APA 7 reference models

The pattern is **Author. (Date). Title. Source.** Sentence case for titles of works;
italics for the title of a stand-alone work or the periodical; DOI or URL last, with no
period after it.

| Source type | Model |
| --- | --- |
| Book | Okafor, A. N. (2021). *Capital allocation in family firms* (2nd ed.). Harbor Press. |
| Chapter in an edited book | Lee, J., & Ortiz, M. (2023). Measuring liquidity. In R. Patel (Ed.), *Guide to credit markets* (pp. 41–66). Harbor Press. |
| Journal article | Chen, L., Ruiz, A., & Novak, P. (2022). Earnings guidance and analyst dispersion. *Journal of Corporate Finance Research, 18*(3), 211–234. https://doi.org/10.0000/jcfr.2022.0183 |
| Newspaper or magazine article online | Brooks, T. (2025, March 4). Regional banks tighten lending. *The Daily Ledger*. https://www.example.com/regional-banks |
| Web page, organization as author | Federal Reserve Bank of Example. (2025, June 10). *Household debt update*. https://www.example.org/household-debt |
| Report by an organization | Northwind Research Institute. (2024). *Mid-market M&A outlook 2025*. https://www.example.org/outlook-2025 |
| Government report | U.S. Example Agency. (2024). *Small business lending survey* (Publication No. 24-117). https://www.example.gov/report-24-117 |
| Data set | Example Statistics Office. (2025). *Quarterly retail sales, 2015–2025* [Data set]. https://www.example.gov/data/retail |
| Video or podcast episode | Rivera, S. (Host). (2024, May 2). Reading a cash-flow statement (No. 48) [Audio podcast episode]. In *Margin Notes*. Example Media. https://www.example.com/ep48 |

**In text:** (Okafor, 2021); (Lee & Ortiz, 2023); (Chen et al., 2022); a direct quotation
adds the page: (Okafor, 2021, p. 13). No date: (Northwind Research Institute, n.d.).

## Finance sources (this repo's models, in APA 7 form)

APA has no single rule for some of these; the models below apply its general pattern, and
the note says what to check.

| Source | Model | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual report on Form 10-K | Northwind Corp. (2025). *Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024*. U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. https://www.sec.gov/[path-to-filing] | Cite the filing on EDGAR, not a summary of it; give the fiscal year |
| Earnings call transcript | Northwind Corp. (2025, February 6). *Q4 2024 earnings call* [Transcript]. Example Transcripts. https://www.example.com/nwc-q4-2024 | Name who published the transcript; quote the speaker by name in the text |
| Terminal or database data | Bloomberg L.P. (2025). *Northwind Corp. EV/EBITDA, 2020–2024* [Data set]. Retrieved March 3, 2025, from Bloomberg Terminal. | A retrieval date, because the data change; many readers can't access the source, so give the figures |
| Analyst report | Lee, J. (2025, January 15). *Northwind Corp.: Initiating at Buy* [Equity research report]. Example Securities. | Usually not public: check your license before quoting it outside your firm |
| Your own interview | (Not in the reference list.) In text: (P. Novak, personal communication, March 2, 2025) | APA treats unrecoverable sources as personal communications |
| Company website or press release | Northwind Corp. (2025, April 1). *Northwind completes acquisition of Fabrikam* [Press release]. https://www.example.com/news/fabrikam | Promotional: verify key figures against the filings |

## One source in three styles

A journal article by three authors:

| Style | Reference-list or bibliography entry | In text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **APA 7** | Chen, L., Ruiz, A., & Novak, P. (2022). Earnings guidance and analyst dispersion. *Journal of Corporate Finance Research, 18*(3), 211–234. https://doi.org/10.0000/jcfr.2022.0183 | (Chen et al., 2022, p. 215) |
| **MLA 9** | Chen, Li, et al. "Earnings Guidance and Analyst Dispersion." *Journal of Corporate Finance Research*, vol. 18, no. 3, 2022, pp. 211–34, https://doi.org/10.0000/jcfr.2022.0183. | (Chen et al. 215) |
| **Chicago 18, author-date** | Chen, Li, Ana Ruiz, and Petra Novak. 2022. "Earnings Guidance and Analyst Dispersion." *Journal of Corporate Finance Research* 18 (3): 211–34. https://doi.org/10.0000/jcfr.2022.0183. | (Chen et al. 2022, 215) |

Chicago's notes-bibliography system puts the citation in a footnote instead; use it when a
teacher or publisher in the humanities asks for it.

## Crediting an openly licensed work (TASL)

For Creative Commons material, Creative Commons recommends giving the **T**itle, **A**uthor,
**S**ource (a link), and **L**icense (a link), plus a note of any changes:
